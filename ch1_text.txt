CHAPTER IPROJECT DESCRIPTIONIntroductionThe success of local ordinances hinges on effective archiving and public input. However, many local government units, such as Bongabong's Sangguniang Bayan (SB), struggle to manage paper-based drafts. Currently, they depend on physical bulletin boards, letters, and printed materials, all of which are prone to disarray. Over time, older documents often become damaged or go missing.Because of this, it becomes very hard to find passed resolutions and track how far along an ordinance is.When local laws are stored properly and easy to track, the public easily trusts the government more. Being transparent helps stop misinformation. On the other hand, messy records make it hard for the community to get involved in what the government is doing. Fixing how these records are kept and shared is important so the local government can function better.For the SB of Bongabong, building a web application can change how they handle their ordinances. A digital platform removes the need to constantly shuffle physical papers or wait for letters to arrive. This also provides a pathway for individuals to participate in public consultations and vote online, regardless of their location in remote barangays, where attending town hall meetings might be impractical. This upgrade makes sure everyone is informed about pending laws.The proposed system fixes these old problems by giving the SB a central online hub. By turning physical folders into a searchable digital archive, the staff won't have a hard time tracking file anymore. Overall, the system speeds up the legislative process and makes sure the public is always updated.Project ContextThis project is an interactive website that is created specifically for the Sangguniang Bayan of Bongabong to maintain and provide information on local ordinances. Rather than passing paper copies of ordinances between different committees, SB officials and the Secretary can view a digital dashboard that shows the real-time status of ordinances from first reading through approval (or enactment). In addition, the project contains a consultation portal as well. Users can easily find laws, review pending ordinances, and access a digital archive by visiting the website without creating an account; however, residents will need to verify their identity through a guest submission form by attaching a valid ID in order to vote (yes, no, or no opinion) and provide feedback securely, without the need for an account.Citizen input has never been so important when it comes to legislative tracking, and this project aims to enhance the connection between local government and the communities they represent, with a particular focus on remote barangays. Many people who wish to participate in their local government's decision-making process, do so by attending town hall meetings; however, due to logistical issues, such as time, distance, and other commitments, many cannot attend town hall meetings. This project will assist citizens in connecting and remaining engaged and informed about local law(s) through visual representation of LOCAL LAWS. The project is designed to provide all local citizens with a clear, concise, and user-friendly manner as they review the following: a comprehensive summary of public votes on each proposed law; official signed law in PDF format; timeline representing how law is passed.Objectives of the StudyThis study, titled "iBOSys: An Interactive Ordinance Consultation and Governance Platform for Sangguniang Bayan of Bongabong," focuses on transactions related to the main objective of the research. It aims to address the following specific objectives:Streamline the tracking of proposed ordinances for the local officials.Setup a secure public consultation from residents.Centralize the storage of approved local laws for easy public access.Consolidate public feedback and votes to assist the committee in decision-making.Improve the communication between the Sangguniang Bayan and the public.Benefits of the StudyThis contains the practical benefits the system provides to the business, users, and technical stakeholders:Lessen the physical storage space and manual effort needed to keep paper documents for the government staff.Speed up the search process for old ordinances by using a digital database that safely flags outdated or amended laws.Widen the reach of public consultations by letting residents in remote areas participate online.Give the public immediate access to newly approved laws through simple digital summaries and downloadable files.Provide the SB Secretary and committee members with clear summaries of public opinion to help them make informed decisions.Scope and LimitationsThe project covers the digitalization of the ordinance tracking process, a searchable archive for approved laws, and a public consultation portal for the Sangguniang Bayan of Bongabong. It includes tracking the law's progress, uploading official PDF documents, and collecting color-coded votes from the community.For the limitations, the study is strictly focused on the local legislative processes of Bongabong, Oriental Mindoro, so it might not immediately fit the setup of other municipalities. Also, the system completely depends on an internet connection. Finally, verifying the true identity of online users relies on checking provided IDs or local barangay records, which means the system is not entirely immune to very sophisticated fake accounts.Operational DefinitionAccount. A registered user profile within the iBOSys platform that grants specific access permissions based on the assigned role, such as System Admin, Secretary, Vice Mayor, or SB Member.Application. A software program developed to perform specific tasks; in this study, it refers to the web-based dashboard and mobile app built for ordinance management and public consultation.Archive. A searchable digital repository within the system where all approved ordinances are permanently stored, allowing the public to retrieve official documents by keyword, date, committee, or sector tag.Civil Society Organization. Groups and sectors inside the municipality that represent specific community viewpoints during the consultations.Dashboard. A visual interface within the web-based platform that displays real-time summaries of ordinance statuses, consultation results, and system notifications for authorized SB staff.Database. An organized digital storage system used to keep and retrieve structured data.Effective. Refers to the date when an approved ordinance officially takes legal force and becomes enforceable within the municipality after completing all required readings and reviews.First Reading. The first step where the title and number of the drafted ordinance are read aloud during an SB session.Flag. A visual indicator or warning badge displayed on an ordinance record within the system to alert users that the law has been amended, outdated, or requires attention.Format. The standardized structure and layout used for encoding ordinance data into the system, including required fields such as the title, proponent, committee, date, and PDF attachment.iBOSys. Refers to the Interactive Ordinance Consultation and Governance Platform created for this project.Interactive. A system feature that allows two-way engagement between the platform and its users, such as casting votes, submitting feedback, and receiving real-time responses during public consultations.Local Government Unit. A political subdivision of the national government responsible for governing a specific locality; in this study, it refers to the municipal government of Bongabong, Oriental Mindoro.Online Hub. A centralized digital location accessible through the internet where all ordinance-related activities, tracking, consultation, voting, and archiving are consolidated into one platform.Ordinance. A formal local law or policy passed by the Sangguniang Bayan.Public Consultation: A process where citizens can give their feedback, suggestions, and votes on a pending local law before it gets approved.Platform. The digital environment through which the system operates and is accessed by users.Real-time. The immediate processing and display of data as events occur, such as the instant updating of vote tallies and consultation results without requiring a page reload.Sangguniang Bayan. The local legislative body of the municipality that drafts and passes local ordinances.Store. The act of saving and preserving data within the system's database for future retrieval.Streamline. To simplify and improve the efficiency of a process by removing unnecessary manual steps.Track. To monitor and record the progress of an ordinance as it moves through each legislative stage from First Reading through committee hearings, readings, and final approval within the system dashboard.Web. A network of interconnected pages and resources accessible through the internet using a browser.Website. A collection of related web pages hosted on the internet and accessible through a browser.
