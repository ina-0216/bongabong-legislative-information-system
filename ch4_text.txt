It identifies the necessary inputs from stakeholders, the technical processes involved in development using the SDLC V-Model, and the final functional outputs delivered to the Sangguniang Bayan of Bongabong.Requirements DocumentationUser Roles and RegistrationThe system has six user roles. The System Admin creates the Secretary's account using a full name, email, and temporary password through the web-based dashboard. The Secretary manages all other accounts and encodes ordinances by inputting the ordinance number, title, proponent, committee, date, and PDF file, all through the web-based dashboard. The Vice Mayor and SB Members are given accounts by the Secretary using their name, position, email, and contact number — the Vice Mayor approves corrections while SB Members only view the dashboard, both through the web-based platform. Members of the general public can seamlessly search for and browse approved ordinances without the need to register for an account. To vote or submit feedback during public consultations, they must use a guest form requiring their name, barangay, contact number, and an attached valid ID or barangay clearance, which the Secretary then approves via the web-based dashboard. No resident account registration is required.SB Member and Vice Mayor accounts are created every three-year term and deactivated when officials leave.Login and AuthenticationThe General Public browses, votes, and submits feedback without logging in, while SB staff log in on desktop. When the Secretary creates an SB Member or Vice Mayor account, the system automatically emails them a temporary password which they must change on first login. Usernames are 6–20 alphanumeric characters; passwords must be at least 8 characters with uppercase, lowercase, a number, and a special character. If an account password is forgotten, staff members can use a secure "Forgot Password" feature to receive a reset link via their registered email.Profile ManagementSB staff can update their contact details through the web-based dashboard. A "Contact Me" button is available on the portal for anyone to reach the SB office. The Secretary activates and deactivates SB Member and Vice Mayor accounts at the start and end of each term through the web-based dashboard.Ordinance ManagementThe Secretary encodes ordinances on desktop — inputting the title, proponent, committee, date, and uploading the PDF — and the system tracks them through: First Reading → Committee Assignment → Committee Hearing → Public Consultation → Second Reading → Third Reading → For Mayor's Signature → For SP Review → Approved. Once approved, the record is permanently locked, but the Secretary can submit a Minor Correction request for clerical errors, which the Vice Mayor must approve in-system before any change is applied and logged in the audit trail.Interactive Public ConsultationThe General Public can vote using Agree, Disagree, or Neutral, and submit written comments through the mobile application or the web-based platform via an ID-verified guest form. Approved votes are tallied in real time using the 50% + 1 or two-thirds majority rule. The Secretary and SB Members can filter comments by barangay, sentiment, or keyword through the web-based dashboard to prepare the Committee Report.Amendatory Cross-ReferencingWhen a law is updated, the Secretary links the new ordinance to the old one on desktop, causing the old ordinance to display a warning badge and a direct link to the newer version so residents always access the most current legislation.VisualizationThe Secretary, Vice Mayor, and SB Members view auto-generated charts on desktop showing real-time consultation pie charts (Agree/Disagree/Neutral), ordinances passed per SB member, ordinances per committee or category, and a year-by-year ordinance trend.ReportingThe system generates downloadable PDF reports on desktop: the Committee Report (consultation results and recommendation), the Annual Ordinance Report (all ordinances in a year with statuses and authors), and the Consultation Participation Report (vote breakdown per ordinance).NotificationThe system sends in-platform notifications through the web-based platform. SB Members and the Vice Mayor are notified when an ordinance is assigned to their committee or a consultation ends; and the Secretary is notified when a guest consultation submission is pending or the Vice Mayor acts on a Minor Correction request.ArchiveThe General Public can search the archive without an account by keyword, date, committee, or sector tag through the mobile application or the web-based platform. Each entry shows the ordinance title, an Approved badge, a Plain Language Summary, a Legislative Timeline, and a downloadable signed PDF, with a warning badge if the ordinance has been amended.
